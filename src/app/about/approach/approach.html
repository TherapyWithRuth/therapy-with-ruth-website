My Approach to Therapy

My approach to therapy is collaborative, supportive, and tailored to your unique experiences and goals. I draw from evidence-based modalities such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT), and EMDR to help clients better understand themselves, navigate difficult emotions, process painful experiences, and develop healthier ways of coping and connecting.

I believe therapy should feel both grounding and empowering. Whether we are working through anxiety, trauma, relationship challenges, life transitions, or patterns that no longer serve you, I strive to create a nonjudgmental space where you can feel supported while building practical tools for meaningful and lasting change.
