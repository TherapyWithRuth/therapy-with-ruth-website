What You Can Expect

I work with individuals, couples, and families navigating periods of stress, uncertainty, transition, and emotional overwhelm. Many of the people I work with are balancing demanding careers, complicated relationships, cultural expectations, or major life changes while trying to maintain a sense of connection to themselves and others.

I strive to create a space where you can show up fully as yourself without fear of judgment or needing to have everything figured out beforehand. Together, we work toward building greater self-understanding, resilience, and a stronger sense of balance in your day-to-day life.
