The Experiences That Shaped Me

As an Afro Latina and the eldest daughter in an immigrant family, I grew up navigating different cultures, expectations, and responsibilities. I often found myself translating not only language, but perspectives as well. Achievement was deeply valued, independence came early, and, like many eldest daughters, I naturally became the helper and the one others relied on. Looking back, those experiences gave me an early appreciation for how complex people really are. They also showed me how much of what we carry goes unseen.
