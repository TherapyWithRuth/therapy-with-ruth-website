Professional Background

I have been working in the field of mental health since 2012. I earned my undergraduate degree from Stony Brook University and my graduate degree from Queens College. Over the years, I’ve worked with individuals, couples, and families navigating anxiety, trauma, eating disorders, body image concerns, ADHD, relationship challenges, life transitions, and the emotional impact of oppression and immigrant experiences.

My work is especially informed by supporting people of color and immigrant communities through a compassionate, trauma-informed, and culturally responsive approach. I strive to create a therapeutic space that feels supportive, collaborative, and grounded in authenticity. My approach integrates evidence-based practices tailored to each person’s unique experiences, needs, and goals while fostering self-worth, resilience, and meaningful personal growth.
